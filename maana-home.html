Higashiyama, Kyoto

Where staying becomes
an act of attention.

Maana is a collection of restored machiya homes, cultural experiences, and a retail atelier in Kyoto's Higashiyama district. Every stay is a private encounter with Japanese craft — earthen walls, hinoki baths, ceramics chosen by hand. Not a hotel. A home that teaches you to slow down.
