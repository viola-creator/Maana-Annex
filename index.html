01

The Intention

Maana Annex is the first home we have built from the ground up using reclaimed materials from our machiya. Since our first home a decade ago, everything we have learned about living, restoring, and making space has been gathered here, shaping a new story that feels wholly our own.

More than just a place to stay, the Annex is a reflection of the stories and people who have inspired us along the way.

意図 — 心を込めた新しい始まり

02

The Hands

The Annex was created with many hands and we wanted that human touch to remain visible in a world shaped by mass production and uniform templates.

You may notice the gentle arc of a plasterer's trowel left in the wall, or a ceramicist's fingerprint pressed into a glaze. The recycled earthen walls, shaped by our sakan, a traditional Japanese plasterer, still carry a faint mineral scent on cool mornings before the room begins to warm.

The Annex is a celebration of process, artistry, and the beauty of natural materials made slowly by human hands.

手の記憶 — すべての表面に宿る人の証

03

Full Circle

The Annex sits next door to the very first machiya we restored. The house where Maana began. After years of working outward, opening new homes across Kyoto, we found ourselves building again on the same street.

For Maana, the Annex is more than a place to stay. It is a continuation of our story, woven together through memory, material, and the people who have walked alongside us over the years. And in welcoming guests here, it feels as though we are opening the door not simply to a stay, but to a homecoming.

円環 — 始まりの場所に帰る

Tsuchikabe — 土壁

A second life for our earthen walls.

The tsuchikabe walls in this home once stood inside Maana Atelier. Carefully taken down by hand, each fragment of earth was cleaned, reclaimed, and mixed again into fresh clay by the Maana team. The same earth can hereby begin a second life in a new home, carrying the memories from Maana forward.
